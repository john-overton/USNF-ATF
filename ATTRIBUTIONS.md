# Attributions

## Terrain imagery

Contains modified Copernicus Sentinel data 2024.

Source: Sentinel-2 Level-2A true-color imagery (10 m red, green and blue bands),
June–August 2024. Public cloud-optimized GeoTIFF access via Element 84 Earth Search
and the AWS Open Data Sentinel-2 collection:
https://registry.opendata.aws/sentinel-2-l2a-cogs/

Legal notice: https://cds.climate.copernicus.eu/licences/ec-sentinel

Modifications: low-cloud scene selection, cloud/shadow masking with the scene
classification layer, temporal-agreement filling of persistent mask gaps, mosaicking, reprojection into the theater's Lambert azimuthal
equal-area coordinates and downsampling into a local RGBA atlas. Source dates and
asset URLs are retained in the generated provenance. Small residual color gaps may
use bounded nearest-valid-color interpolation; its extent is recorded separately. Authoritative terrain water
polygons supply a flat water color where satellite coverage is unavailable.
Optional coastal texture repair reflects nearby interior land colors into a bounded
shore strip, with nearest-color fallback and a feathered landward edge. This synthetic
padding does not change the terrain elevations or water classification.
Optional simplified color maps average land samples into artistic appearance
weights and apply separately authored seasonal palettes. They are not verified
land-cover or seasonal observations.
This is not a live satellite feed or a claim of uniform acquisition date.

These credits are available in the app's About / Data credits disclosure and in
the terrain manifest. No European Union, ESA or data-provider endorsement is implied.

## Terrain elevation and water masks

Copernicus DEM GLO-30 2021 (ESA), including its water body mask (WBM).
Source dataset and license information: https://registry.opendata.aws/copernicus-dem/

Modifications: reprojection, filtered elevation levels, quantized terrain chunks,
water-polygon extraction and bounded coastline smoothing. Water classification
comes from the source mask, not elevation alone. Dataset-specific notices and
provenance remain in each generated theater's manifest and pipeline records.

## Moon texture

`engine/src/terrain/assets/moon-nasa-galileo.png` is a 384 px derivative of
NASA/JPL/USGS's Galileo full-moon image, **PIA00405**, retrieved from
https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Full_moon.png on 2026-09-11. It is a
United States government work in the public domain. The bundled derivative is
SHA-256 `e30eb3aeee0a02737058766560e3e6c4b25f98ee166b2dc4d9c596a53d076d71`.
It is used only as the moon-disc texture; phase, placement and
luminance remain simulation-driven.

Shoreline classification uses modified Copernicus imagery and DEM/WBM as artistic
appearance hints. It is not a verified geological survey. The bundled shoreline
material swatches are original deterministic artwork generated by
`tools/terrain/bake-shoreline-texture.py`; they contain no satellite or retail pixels.
